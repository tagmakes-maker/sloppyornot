Your site works. ClearPath makes it work for everyone.

One script tag audits your page, writes alt text for images, fixes low-contrast text, and makes every button and filter work from the keyboard. Then it shows you exactly what it changed.

  • Alt text that says something

    Every image gets a short, specific description, and you can edit any of them.

  • Contrast you can read

    Text that fails contrast checks gets darker or larger until it passes.

  • Keyboard all the way down

    Clickable divs become real buttons, with a focus ring you can see.

  • A before and after report

    See what a screen reader heard before ClearPath, and what it hears now.
